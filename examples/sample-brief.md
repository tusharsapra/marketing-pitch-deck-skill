# Fictional syntax example: Driftlight

This is fictional supplied material for demonstrating the blueprint and calculation utilities. It is not research or a validated pitch strategy.

Driftlight is a fictional adjustable reading-lamp brand. Its supplied product description says brightness can be adjusted with a dial. The planning exercise targets adult readers in two fictional priority cities. The objective is consideration and qualified enquiries, without a historical performance baseline.

Illustrative media-only scenarios use INR 300,000 and INR 500,000. The CPM of INR 100 and CPV of INR 0.50 are invented inputs for arithmetic demonstration. Inventory is proposed. Forecast figures are illustrative and are not estimates of real market performance. Production, fees and tax are outside these sample budgets.

The four-slide JSON is a schema example rather than a complete recommended marketing deck.
