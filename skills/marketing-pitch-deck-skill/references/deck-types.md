# Adapt the deck to the brief

| Brief type | Emphasize | Additional checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Relevant audience, reach/attention roles, memorable product truth, frequency and brand measurement | Define view metrics; separate average frequency from exposure distribution |
| Product launch | Product benefit/proof, availability, priority cohorts, launch window and recognizable executions | Verify exact product identity, distribution and launch dependencies |
| Acquisition | Demand creation/capture, barriers, landing pages, qualified outcomes and sales handoff | Label baseline-free forecasts; define qualified outcomes and operational capacity |
| B2B or trade | Buyer, influencer/specifier/user roles, proof, adoption and sales cycle | Keep trade and consumer messages distinct; connect media to business follow-up |
| Portfolio | Product prioritization, shared versus separate journeys and allocation | Avoid repeating identical mini-decks; make prioritization explicit |
| Multiple markets | Local evidence, language, availability and scenario allocation | Do not extrapolate global data without justification |
| Partner/integration | Role, audience signal, placement mechanic, product presence and feasibility | Separate proposed inventory from confirmed terms; verify timing/rights |
| Research-only request | Scoped findings, evidence and strategic implications | Deliver the requested research; do not force production of a pitch |
| Visual-only revision | Current master, locked copy/order and visual clarity | Preserve approved strategy, numbers, sources and exclusions |

Add credentials, commercial scope, team or case studies only when requested or useful and supported by supplied facts. Do not invent an agency's experience or borrow the creator's pitch count as the client's credentials.

Treat a cultural property as an entry point when it serves the brand's audience and window; it need not become the entire brand story. Respect slide limits. A useful deck can be short when the brief is focused; a complete workflow does not require every research module to appear on a slide.
