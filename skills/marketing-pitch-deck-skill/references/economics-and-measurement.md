# Economics and measurement

## Define cost and delivery bases

Record currency, net/gross basis, media, production, fees, taxes, contingencies and the source/date for rates. Define impressions, view thresholds, clicks, leads and qualified outcomes for each format. Do not compare completed views with starts as if they are identical. Preserve source units when using values quoted per thousand, lakh, million or percentage points.

Use these formulas only with compatible scope and definitions:

| Calculation | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| CPM impressions | media spend / CPM × 1,000 |
| CPV views | media spend / CPV |
| CPC clicks | media spend / CPC |
| CPA outcomes | media spend / CPA |
| Click forecast | impressions × CTR fraction |
| Conversion forecast | clicks × CVR fraction |
| Average frequency | impressions / deduplicated unique reach |
| Blended CPM | comparable spend / comparable impressions × 1,000 |
| Blended CPV | comparable spend / comparable views |
| Cost per qualified outcome | relevant cost / qualified outcomes |

Use CTR/CVR as fractions in machine-readable models: 0.01 means 1%. State the denominator. Do not average rates without weighting, add overlapping platform reach as total unique reach, or treat average frequency as the percentage of people receiving a chosen number of exposures. Frequency-distribution goals need a distribution estimate or platform measurement, not only an average.

Label forecasts and illustrative rates as assumptions. No forecast guarantees delivery or commercial outcomes. A partner offer demonstrates an offer; it does not prove effectiveness. Validate rate cards and attribution settings before committing spend.

## Design scenarios and phasing

Ensure each scenario reconciles to the stated budget basis. Show what the increment buys: additional markets, qualified reach, attention, formats, production or repetition. Do not multiply every line automatically. Explain opportunity cost and dependency when optional properties are included.

Phase activity around the actual campaign and decision window. Include asset development, landing-page readiness, approvals, booking and sales preparation. Use phase entry conditions if dates are missing. Update every slide and summary when a number or allocation changes.

## Measure the decision

For each priority KPI state definition, baseline or absence of one, data source, reporting cadence, target/assumption and optimization decision. Match the primary outcome to the brief. For acquisition connect media actions to qualified leads and eventual offline/sales outcomes. Define qualification and handoff rather than counting every lead equally.

For awareness consider audience quality, deduplicated reach where possible, frequency distribution, completed viewing or attention with clear definitions, and an appropriate brand-impact study where feasible. Branded search can be one signal; movement alone does not prove campaign-caused recall or revenue. State measurement gaps that materially change interpretation.

Use the bundled validator to catch reference and arithmetic errors. It cannot determine whether rates are realistic, scopes match, strategy is sound or source evidence proves the claim. Review those manually.
