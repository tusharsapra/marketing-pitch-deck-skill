# Media and executions

## Give media a job before choosing partners

Define the required roles: broad qualified reach, attention, contextual relevance, proof, demand capture, retargeting or qualified action. Then shortlist environments that can perform them for the priority cohorts. Keep the plan concentrated enough for investment and measurement to matter; a long platform list is not a strategy.

For each material partner record:

- Role, cohort, geography and supporting audience signal/source.
- Placement, format and exact proposed execution.
- Targeting or contextual method and its limitations.
- Inventory status, availability, specs, lead time, approvals and reporting.
- Price/cost model, metric definition, assumptions and commercial exclusions.
- Likely audience overlap, deduplication limits and incremental contribution.

Distinguish current proposal evidence from confirmed booking. A named show, platform feature or event integration is proposed until inventory, timing and rights are confirmed. Do not imply permission or availability from an attractive mockup. Draft outstanding partner questions privately; do not send messages unless the user explicitly instructs it.

Explain how the shortlist was derived. Unsupported audience claims prevent a confident targeting promise, but they do not prevent a clearly labelled exploratory recommendation. Do not prescribe the same frequency for every partner or add overlapping reach as unique reach.

## Make ideas tangible

Select the lead idea and distinguish supporting executions. For each, describe audience tension, product benefit, mechanic, placement, exact product identity, user experience, desired action, time window, dependencies and success signal.

Show the idea with a placement view, storyboard or mockup. Use full product names, genuine product proportions and recognizable identity. Explain the moment of integration and what a user sees or does. A tiny logo on generic stock imagery does not demonstrate an integration. For interactive ideas show the trigger, interaction and result. For content integrations show the scene or episode role and duration where known.

Verify named events, series and dates using current official sources. If uncertain, mark the property/timeframe proposed and specify the condition for selection. Build a fallback mechanic when unconfirmed inventory is essential to the plan.

If one property becomes the lead idea, propagate that choice through media roles, partner slides, investment, phasing and the close. Do not leave the campaign idea detached from the media architecture. If the idea is removed, update dependent slides and assets.

Use generated imagery only to illustrate a proposed concept, never to establish that a campaign ran. Use labels when a reader could mistake a concept for real activity. Use exact logos and product artwork from genuine assets rather than generating approximations.
