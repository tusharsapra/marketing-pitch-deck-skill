# Marketing Pitch Deck Skill

Turn the website and brief into a researched, coherent and editable pitch the client can understand without missing presenter explanations. Build quality checks into the work; do not require a separate manager-feedback cycle to finish.

## Start

1. Read the supplied brief, website and current files. Identify the intended decision, geography, audiences, budget basis, timing, format/count and constraints. Use [brief and brand](references/brief-and-brand.md).
2. Check available research, image, presentation and rendering capabilities. Prefer the environment's existing presentation skill/toolchain for production. Read [tools and delivery](references/tools-and-delivery.md); do not assume a paid account, special connector or API is available.
3. Choose the appropriate scope using [deck types](references/deck-types.md). Ask only material missing questions and progress independent work. Record permitted assumptions; avoid an unnecessary approval after every stage.
4. For existing-deck work, establish the latest version, excluded content and locked copy/order before editing. Route visual-only work directly through the revision, design and verification rules.

Resolve bundled paths relative to this installed skill. Optionally initialize working files with `python3 <skill-directory>/scripts/init_project.py <project-directory>`. Keep project outputs outside the installed skill.

## Work through the full method

### 1. Decode the brief and audit product truth

Create a brief map, brand/product fact sheet and asset inventory. Separate supplied facts, objectives, hypotheses and commitments. Verify exact names, benefits, proof, availability and conversion path. Make the objective clear and concise.

### 2. Research the decisions

Create a research plan tied to choices, then examine relevant market/local context, demand and observable competition. Read [research and competition](references/research-and-competition.md). Maintain source/claim IDs, data period, market, units, observation dates and limitations. Use `verified`, `supplied`, `directional`, `assumption` and `proposed` accurately.

Preserve comparable settings for indexed demand data. Do not convert relative search interest into market share, absolute demand, recall or spend. Do not infer competitor performance, targeting or budget from visible ads. Pair analysis with usable evidence.

### 3. Define audience and strategy

Use [audience and strategy](references/audience-and-strategy.md). Profile cohorts, triggers, barriers, proof, relevant locations, decision roles and journeys before choosing platforms. Distinguish audience definitions from feasible buying signals. Synthesize the tension, credible brand right to win and the behavior/belief change. Trace evidence to choice, execution and metric.

### 4. Write the larger story before slides

Use [narrative and slide writing](references/narrative-and-slide-writing.md). Draft the argument in prose, then chapter order, header sequence and slide blueprint. Respect the requested slide count, including cover/appendix unless stated otherwise. Give each slide one job and a supported value-conveying header. Use direct professional language and purposeful visuals; remove repetition.

### 5. Give media clear roles

Use [media and executions](references/media-and-executions.md). Define roles before partner names. Qualify each material partner against cohort, geography, inventory, specs, timing, price, measurement and overlap. Show the source or assumption behind selection. Keep proposed properties distinct from confirmed commercial availability.

### 6. Make executions visible and practical

Specify the lead idea, product benefit, placement mechanic, exact product identity, user experience, action, window and dependencies. Create a mockup, storyboard or placement view that lets the client picture it. Verify named events and dates. Propagate the chosen idea through partner slides, budget, phasing and the close.

### 7. Reconcile investment and measurement

Use [economics and measurement](references/economics-and-measurement.md). Model cost bases, allocations, forecast assumptions and incremental scenario choices. Define metrics and denominators. Do not add overlapping reach as unique reach or treat average frequency as an exposure distribution. Connect acquisition to qualified outcomes and operational handoff. Update every dependent number when allocations change.

### 8. Produce the editable presentation

Use [design and production](references/design-and-production.md) and the available presentation toolchain. Follow the current master and official assets. Keep core text, tables and analytical charts editable. Choose layouts by content, use meaningful evidence and exact product artwork, and respect current screenshot instructions. Do not insert creator attribution into client decks.

### 9. Verify and repair

Read [quality and revisions](references/quality-and-revisions.md) before drafting and again before handoff. Apply brief, evidence, story, commercial/math, visual/technical and revision-integrity passes. Render and inspect every slide individually and as a montage. Repair root causes and dependencies. Structural checks do not replace evidence or visual judgment.

If using the bundled JSON blueprint, read [plan schema](references/plan-schema.md) and run `scripts/validate_plan.py`. Inspect the produced PPTX with `scripts/inspect_pptx.py` where applicable. State precisely when a check could not be performed.

### 10. Deliver the complete requested outcome

Provide the editable deck, requested preview/exports and relevant supporting model or sources. Keep internal logs out of client-facing slides. Briefly identify material assumptions, proposed inventory and outstanding execution dependencies. Do not stop at a helpful outline when production tools are available.

## Completion standard

Ensure that the client can understand the story, research supports the choices, priority cohorts and media have clear roles, executions visibly connect to the product, economics reconcile, uncertainty is clear, current constraints are preserved and the actual deck has been inspected. Keep private client evidence in its authorized project; bundle no private data into this reusable skill.
